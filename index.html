Projects

Bugu Sport

Role: Project Leader | Responsibilities: Project management, full-stack development, operations & maintenance, market research, and promotion

Overview: Bugu Sport is a WeChat Mini Program designed for campus sports, primarily targeting college and K-12 students. Key features include game invitations, streetball participation, team management, competition organization, and user authentication. The platform has attracted 1,400+ visitors and 600+ registered users.

Recognition: National Second Prize & South China First Prize in China Collegiate Computing Contest, and Third Prize in the "ByteDance" Cup Software Design Competition.

Intellectual Property: Source code registered with the Copyright Protection Center of China (CPCC).
